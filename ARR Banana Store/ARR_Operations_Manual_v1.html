One person is named for each job every day. If a job has no name, it is the Senior's job.

Opening (5:00 AM)

  • Open the store, turn on lights and fans, check the floor and display are clean.
  • Check every banana on display. Remove black, moldy or crushed fruit and put it in the spoilage crate.
  • Set up the display: oldest ripe stock in front, fresher stock at the back.
  • Count the opening cash (float) with a second person watching. Write the amount and both names in the cash notebook.
  • Check the scale reads zero with nothing on it.

Receiving a delivery

Most losses happen here. Do not accept a delivery without checking it.

  • Count the crates and compare with what Archie said is coming. If the count is wrong, stop and message Archie before unloading.
  • Weigh at least 1 crate out of every 5. Write each weight down.
  • If the average is more than 1 kg below the standard crate weight, take a photo of the scale and message Archie.
  • Check quality: bruising, overripe, rotten. If more than 1 crate in 10 is bad, take photos and message Archie before storing.
  • Write the date on every crate.
  • Store new crates behind old ones. Do not mix new and old stock.
  • Message Archie on Telegram: "DELIVERY — date — number of crates — kg weighed — problems found."

Archie enters the delivery (kg, amount paid, trucking) in the Command Center. Staff do not.

Stock transfer to Rizal

Every kilo that leaves Main for Rizal must be written down, or Rizal's profit cannot be known.

  • Weigh what is sent, by fruit.
  • Write it in the transfer notebook: date, fruit, kg, who sent it.
  • The Rizal staff weighs it on arrival and signs next to it. If the weights differ by more than 1 kg, message Archie.
  • Send a photo of the signed notebook page to the Telegram group.

Selling, prices and discounts

  • Sell at the posted price. The scale faces the customer. Say the total out loud ("₱180 po").
  • Follow the Service Process Manual for greeting and customer care.
  • Wholesale or reseller price: only the prices on Archie's posted wholesale list. Any lower price needs Archie's approval before the sale.
  • Every GCash payment: check the confirmation on the store phone before the customer leaves.
  • No utang (credit) without Archie's approval.

Ripening (ethrel soaking)

  • Soak only what will be displayed within 2 days.
  • Label every soaked batch with the date.
  • Large batches (more than [confirm] crates) need Archie's approval first.

Spoilage

  • Remove bad fruit from display as soon as it is seen.
  • Nothing is thrown away before it is weighed and written in the spoilage notebook: date, fruit, kg.
  • At closing, multiply kg by the cost per kg on the posted cost list. That peso amount goes in the daily report as "Spoilage at cost".
  • Customer replacements count as spoilage. Write "replacement" next to them.

Cash and closing count

Count at closing, every day, at each branch. Two people watch the count when two are present.

Expected cash

Expected cash in the drawer = opening float + cash sales − expenses paid from the drawer − vale paid from the drawer.

GCash sales are not in the drawer. Count them separately from the GCash history.

Cash short or over

Cash short or over = cash counted − expected cash.

  • Within ₱200 either way: write it in the report, nothing else to do.
  • More than ₱200 short or over: write the reason in the Notes column of the daily report. "Hindi ko alam" is not a reason; check for unrecorded sales, wrong change, unrecorded expenses or vale.
  • Over ₱200 on 3 days in one month for the same person: Archie talks with that person directly.

A surplus is also an error. It usually means a sale or a payment was not recorded.

After the count

  • Set aside the float for tomorrow.
  • The rest goes to Archie or the safe as Archie instructs [confirm].
  • Write the closing count and both names in the cash notebook.

Month-end stock count

On the last day of every month, after closing:

  • Weigh everything left, by fruit, at each branch: display, storage and soaking.
  • Enter it in the Stock Count tab: month, branch, fruit, kg on hand, cost per kg.
  • Archie checks the count the next morning.

Without this count, the month's profit is only an estimate.

Discipline

  • First time a rule is broken: verbal reminder from the Senior.
  • Second time in the same month: written warning, signed.
  • Third time in the same month: meeting with Archie.

No salary is deducted for shortages or mistakes as a penalty. The only salary deductions are vale repayments under the signed authorization, and anything else Archie confirms is allowed after checking with DOLE or an accountant.

Open the Dashboard of the ARR Command Center and look only at the red cells:

  • Days cash was short by more than ₱200: read the Notes, ask about that specific day.
